    Before you start: notation & how these boards work
    • r4c7 means row 4, column 7. Rows are numbered top to bottom, columns left to right.
    • Small digits are candidates (pencil marks): the values a cell can still take.
    • Every board here already has the basic techniques applied: naked/hidden singles, locked candidates, naked/hidden pairs & triples. So when you see a board, the easy moves are used up and the advanced pattern is what's left.
    • Bold black digits are the puzzle's givens. Blue digits were filled in by the basic techniques.
    • A cell sees another cell if they share a row, a column or a box.
